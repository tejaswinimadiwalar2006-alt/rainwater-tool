Installation & Planning Guide

Steps for Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting

  1. Catchment Inspection: Clean the roof surface thoroughly and repair any cracks.
  2. Gutter Integration: Attach UV-resistant PVC gutters along slopes.
  3. First-Flush Installation: Install a manual or automatic divert pipe.
  4. Filter Connection: Direct diverted water into a sand-gravel filter unit.
  5. Tank Storage & Recharge: Direct filtered water into a tank or recharge pit.

Importance of Rainwater Harvesting

Conserves Groundwater

Reduces over-reliance on local borewells and allows natural aquifers to replenish.

Saves Utility Bills

Fulfills non-potable needs like gardening and cleaning, reducing water expenses.

Prevents Soil Erosion

Capturing rooftop runoff prevents surface erosion and protects foundations.
