Tank Sizing & System Design

First Flush Diverter

Always route the initial 1–2 mm of rainfall away from the storage tank. This flushes away dust, debris, and bird droppings collected on the rooftop.

Storage Tank Sizing

Recommended minimum tank capacity is roughly 20–25% of annual harvest potential to store water effectively across non-rainy periods.

Filtration Chamber

Integrate a multi-layer filter consisting of coarse sand, gravel, and charcoal prior to tank entry to maintain clean stored water.

Installation & Planning Guide

Steps for Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting

  1. Catchment Inspection: Clean the roof surface thoroughly and repair any cracks or leakages.
  2. Gutter Integration: Attach UV-resistant PVC gutters along roof slopes to capture runoff.
  3. First-Flush Installation: Install a manual or automatic divert pipe before the storage inlet.
  4. Filter Connection: Direct diverted water into a sand-gravel filter unit.
  5. Tank Storage & Recharge: Direct filtered water into an above-ground tank or an underground groundwater recharge pit.
